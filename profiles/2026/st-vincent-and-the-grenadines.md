St. Vincent and the Grenadines carries government gross debt of 107.7% of GDP and unemployment of 18.0%. Its economy reached US$1.3 billion in 2025, holding 186th place between Samoa and St. Kitts and Nevis, and is smaller than any U.S. metro area, at 50% of Eagle Pass, TX. Real growth was 3.4%; the 8.5% US$ rise was mostly exchange-rate and price effects.

Services make up 66.4% of output, industry 15.1% and agriculture 3.4%, with manufacturing at 3.9%. GDP per capita of US$12,562 ranks 82nd of 196, and 90th on a purchasing-power basis, for a population of 99,924. Inflation was 0.8%. The country ranked 178th in 2000, when GDP was US$428 million, and output has grown 2.9 times in US$ terms since. The IMF projects GDP of US$1.5 billion by 2030, up 22%, which would lift it to 179th.
