Macao produces GDP per capita of US$75,902, 10th in the world, and on a PPP basis it climbs to 5th, at $137,031 international dollars, with a population of just 685,900. By total output it held 95th place in 2025, between Tunisia and Cambodia.

US$ GDP rose 5.2% to US$52.1 billion, with real growth of +4.7%. The economy runs almost entirely on services, which made up 92.0% of output as of 2024; industry accounted for 4.7% and manufacturing just 0.6%. Inflation stood at 0.3% and unemployment at 2.4%, and government gross debt is 0.0% of GDP.

The dollar economy remains below its 2018 peak of US$55.1 billion, though it has grown 7.7 times since 2000, when it measured US$6.8 billion and ranked 106th. It is about the size of Wyoming. The IMF projects GDP of US$66.8 billion by 2030, a +28% rise, with a forecast ranking of 95th.
