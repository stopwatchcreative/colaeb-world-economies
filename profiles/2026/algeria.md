Algeria held 52nd place in 2025, between Nigeria and Greece, with US$ GDP up 6.6% to US$287.0 billion and real growth of +3.8%. The economy is about the size of Nevada.

Industry carries heavy weight, at 34.8% of output, alongside services at 45.7% and agriculture at 14.5%. Manufacturing accounted for 9.5% of GDP as of 2024. Inflation was low at 1.4%, but unemployment was 11.6% of the labor force. Government gross debt stands at 52.1% of GDP, according to the IMF.

The economy is 5.2 times its 2000 size of US$54.8 billion, when it ranked 50th. With a population of 47.4 million, GDP per capita is US$6,051, 114th of 196, and $18,586 international dollars in PPP terms, 107th. It is the 7th largest economy in the Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan region. The IMF projects GDP of US$338.2 billion by 2030, up 18%, which would keep Algeria 52nd.
