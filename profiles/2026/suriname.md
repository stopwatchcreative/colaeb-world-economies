Suriname's forecast stands out: the IMF projects GDP of US$13.1 billion by 2030, a rise of +191% from US$4.5 billion in 2025, which would lift it to 146th among economies with forecasts. For now it ranks 164th, down 1 place, between Djibouti and Andorra, and remains below its 2014 peak of US$5.2 billion.
