Fiji recorded falling consumer prices in 2025, with inflation at -1.4%. Real GDP grew 3.2% and US$ GDP rose 3.9% to US$6.2 billion, leaving the country unchanged at 159th, between Sierra Leone and South Sudan.

Services account for 57.0% of output, agriculture 14.0% and industry 13.9%, with manufacturing at 9.1%. Unemployment was 5.3% and government gross debt 77.1% of GDP.

With a population of 933,154, output per person is US$6,642, 107th of 196, and $16,338 international dollars on a purchasing-power basis, 113th. Fiji is the 21st largest of 32 economies in East Asia & Pacific and about the size of the Johnstown, PA metro area.

Over the long run, GDP has grown 3.7x since 2000, when it stood at US$1.7 billion and ranked 151st of 194. The IMF projects US$8.0 billion by 2030, a rise of +29% that would place Fiji 155th among economies with forecasts.
