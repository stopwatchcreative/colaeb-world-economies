Oman's economy reached US$109.6 billion in 2025, a shade below its 2022 peak of US$109.9 billion. US$ GDP rose 2.3%, in line with real growth of +2.4%, but the country slipped 1 place to 72nd as Ghana moved ahead; Sri Lanka sits just behind.

Industry is the largest sector, at 52.7% of output, while services account for 47.2% and agriculture 2.8%; manufacturing contributes 9.4% of GDP. Inflation was 1.0% and unemployment 3.2%. Government gross debt stands at 34.7% of GDP, according to the IMF.

The economy is 4.9 times its 2000 size of US$22.3 billion, when it ranked 64th. With a population of 5.5 million, GDP per capita is US$19,947, 62nd of 196, and $42,295 international dollars in PPP terms, 61st. Oman is the 12th largest of 22 economies in the Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan & Pakistan region and about the size of the Bridgeport, CT metro area. The IMF projects GDP of US$132.6 billion by 2030, up 21%, which would place it 78th. Muscat is the capital.
