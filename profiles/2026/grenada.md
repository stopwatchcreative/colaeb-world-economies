Grenada's economy is smaller than any U.S. metro area, at 56% of Eagle Pass, TX. Real growth of +4.4% and a +5.1% rise in US$ GDP brought output to US$1.4 billion, holding Grenada at 183rd, between the Solomon Islands and Vanuatu. Services make up 64.9% of output, industry 15.5%, manufacturing 3.3% and agriculture 2.5%. Inflation was low at 0.6%, while government gross debt stood at 69.4% of GDP.

With a population of 117,303, GDP per capita reaches US$12,107, 83rd of 196. On a PPP basis it falls to 96th, at $21,346 international dollars. In 2000, GDP stood at US$520 million and Grenada ranked 176th; US$ GDP has since grown 2.7 times. The 32nd-largest of 35 economies in Latin America & Caribbean, it is projected by the IMF to grow +32% to US$1.9 billion by 2030, which would place it 175th among the 188 economies with forecasts.
