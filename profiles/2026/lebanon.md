Lebanon climbed 4 places to 111th, but the gain owed little to output. Real GDP grew 4.0% in 2025, while dollar GDP jumped 32.8% to US$34.5 billion: exchange-rate and price effects make up the difference. Inflation ran at 14.6%.

The economy remains well short of its 2018 peak of US$54.9 billion. Government gross debt stands at 139.4% of GDP, according to the IMF, and the IMF does not publish a forecast for Lebanon in this edition.

GDP per person was US$5,897, 116th of 196, and $11,958 international dollars on a purchasing-power basis, 130th, across a population of 5.8 million. In 2000 Lebanon's GDP was US$17.3 billion and it ranked 75th of 194; in dollar terms the economy is 2.0 times that size today. It now sits between El Salvador and Bosnia and Herzegovina, about the size of the Ann Arbor, MI metro area.
