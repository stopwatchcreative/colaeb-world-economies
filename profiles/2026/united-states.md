The United States produced 26.00% of world GDP in 2025, holding 1st place of 196 economies with output of US$30.77 trillion, a rank it also held in 2000 and the IMF expects it to keep in 2030. Real growth ran at 2.2%, while US$ GDP rose 5.0% from US$29.30 trillion in 2024. China sits directly below it in 2nd.

As of 2021, services accounted for 76.3% of output, industry 17.6% and agriculture 0.9%, with manufacturing at 10.5%. GDP per capita reached US$90,027, 8th in the world, though the country ranks 12th on a purchasing-power basis. Inflation stood at 2.7% and unemployment at 4.2%, while government gross debt reached 124.4% of GDP. Output has grown 3.0 times in US$ terms since 2000, when it stood at US$10.25 trillion. The IMF projects GDP of US$38.60 trillion by 2030, a 25% increase.
