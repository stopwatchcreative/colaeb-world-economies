Andorra has just 82,904 people, yet it produces GDP per capita of US$54,292, 24th in the world, and 19th on a PPP basis. By total output it rose 1 place to 165th in 2025, between Suriname and Aruba.

US$ GDP rose 11.3% to US$4.5 billion, while real growth was +3.9%; most of the dollar gain was exchange-rate and price effects, not output. Services account for 77.8% of output, industry 12.8% and agriculture 0.5%, with manufacturing at 3.1% as of 2024. Inflation stood at 2.4%, unemployment at 1.3% and government gross debt at 30.3% of GDP.

The dollar economy has grown 3.1 times since 2000, when it measured US$1.4 billion and ranked 152nd. It is the 51st-largest of 52 economies in Europe & Central Asia and about the size of the Walla Walla, WA metro area. The IMF projects GDP of US$5.7 billion by 2030, a +27% rise that would lift it to 161st.
