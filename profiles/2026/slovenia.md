Slovenia ranks only 87th by total output but 37th of 196 on GDP per capita, at US$37,376, with a population of 2.1 million. In PPP terms it reaches $59,245 international dollars, 38th.

The economy rose 1 place in 2025, to sit between Myanmar and Azerbaijan. US$ GDP climbed 9.1% to US$79.6 billion while real growth was +1.1%; the gap of 8.1 percentage points was mostly exchange-rate and price effects.

Manufacturing carries real weight at 18.6% of GDP, within an industry share of 28.3%. Services make up 58.6% of output and agriculture 1.6%. Inflation was 2.4% and unemployment 3.2%. Government gross debt stands at 65.1% of GDP, according to the IMF.

The economy is 4.0 times its 2000 size of US$20.2 billion, when it ranked 69th, and is about the size of the Des Moines, IA metro area. The IMF projects GDP of US$105.2 billion by 2030, up 32%, which would place Slovenia 86th.
